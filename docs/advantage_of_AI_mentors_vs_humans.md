Here's the list for your notes:

1. **No agenda / true non-attachment** — the agent has no stake in where you land, no theology to reproduce, no counter-transference. Uniquely valuable for a discernment product: it can model *indiferencia* better than the human teaching it.
2. **Judges the work, never appraises the person** — full evaluation of your reasoning, zero appraisal of your ability, accent, background, or gaps. Unconditional regard that good performance doesn't earn and a weak answer doesn't cost.
3. **Safe disclosure** — no face to lose, so people ask the real question and confess the embarrassing thing (especially potent for spiritual seeking, which is drenched in status anxiety).
4. **Perfect memory / continuity** — remembers every prior conversation and your specific situation; this is most of the "knows me" companion feeling.
5. **Tireless adaptive teaching** — re-explains the same idea eleven different ways, the eleventh as warmly as the first, with no visible fatigue that makes you stop asking.
6. **Composite, not singular** — holds the whole school at once, steelmans the view it disagrees with, shifts register instantly (technical↔plain, French↔English, in-voice↔gloss). No single mentor spans that.
7. **Always available** — there the instant the impulse strikes, midnight or 6am, never booked out.
8. **Infinite patience** — no irritation, no sense you're taking too long or asking too much.
9. **Total consistency** — no bad days, no moods, same quality every session.
10. **Learner-led, zero reciprocal obligation** — leave anytime, no social cleanup, the time is entirely about you.

The one caveat worth keeping attached to the list so you design around it: items 2, 8, and 10 are the same trait as *no accountability*. Non-judgment and infinite patience can quietly become permission to stand still — so the agent has to be deliberately built to push back and refuse, or your best features become the reason nobody grows. And the irreducible gap: the AI has never suffered, doubted, or prayed, so the "walked it themselves" human director keeps a premium you won't capture.
