# Been playing frankenstein, it's crazy

Play God and build your own Ai coach

I've been fascinatied by Voice agents.
We're used to chatting our life away with chatgpt or Claude.
and some of us have tried using the voice mode. The humanizing factor is insane.
The frontier between machine and human truly blurs after sharing your daily troubles with the AI.

Voice agents will soon fill our lives.
We already suffer from massive spam calls. I stopped answering calls coming from a French landline anymore for fear of hearing the dreaded "Allo oui ?".
For call centers, the economics are compelling: cost per call drops from 4$ to 1$ or 2$.
Voice Agents can capture information, answer queries and they never tire.
The tech is strong, the voices are pleasant. The latency is low enough to make the conversation really smooth.

My own experiences over the week end were enlightening. There's magic in talking to these entities that go well beyond text messaging.
The voices are warm, insipre trust, and are difficlut to resist
they are convincing. It's difficult to say no!

One of my first agent had a simple task to collect my data and information about me. A basic system prompt was enough to make it difficult to resist. Scale that to thousands of calls and you get a powerful tool for data collection.
Another agent role was to convine me about the price of eggs. That was the first thing that came through my head for a simple non sensitive task. She did the job. with solid references and data points. Most of all she stayed on point. and would only talk about the price of eggs.

Creating a voice agent is very simple and technically straightforward especially with the help of Claude code, Codex and their friends. The pipecat framework glues together the speech to text, LLM, and text to speech. Selevarl providers of transcription or voices are necessary on top of your usual LLM Api. PIpecat also handles context, tooling for sub agents and monitoring. The whole setup is surprisingly easy and the results are impressive.

The possibilities are potentially endless. The challenge is less about the tech stack and the system prompt that defines the persona but about the legal and social framework that shapes viable applications.
A simple search shows that Life Coaches that deal with food issues, religious themes or self help of all kind may not be acceptable. Too many legal and ethical concerns.

I personnaly feel that the benefits are huge. Talking to an AI inseatd of a human has many advantages. The AI being neutral and non judgemental being the first one that comes to mind.
But many people will simply hate talking to AIs. and there will be a huge counter AI mood.
